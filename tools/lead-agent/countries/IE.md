## Where to look (public pages only) — Ireland
- WebSearch: "lift company {{REGION}}", "lift maintenance {{REGION}}", "lift servicing {{REGION}}",
  "lift installation {{REGION}}", "elevator repair {{REGION}} Ireland", also with nearby town names.
- Directories and trade bodies: Golden Pages (goldenpages.ie), LEIA members based in Ireland, CRO
  (Companies Registration Office) listings that surface in search results, business pages in search
  results. Open the page with WebFetch and take the company data from there.
- Where possible open the company's OWN website (contact / about page, usually a .ie domain) and
  verify the trading name, phone, generic e-mail (info@, sales@…), address and the counties they cover.
  Prefer companies with a website and a corporate e-mail.
