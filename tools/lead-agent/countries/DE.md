## Where to look (public pages only) — Germany
- Search in German: "Aufzugsfirma {{REGION}}", "Aufzugswartung {{REGION}}", "Aufzugservice {{REGION}}",
  "Aufzugbau {{REGION}}", "Aufzugsnotdienst {{REGION}}", "Aufzugsanlagen Wartung {{REGION}}", also with
  nearby town names.
- Directories and trade bodies: VFA-Interlift member directory (vfa-interlift.de), Gelbe Seiten,
  11880.com, Das Örtliche, wlw.de (Wer liefert was), business pages in search results. Open the page
  with WebFetch and take the company data from there.
- Every German company website has an "Impressum" page: it states the legal name with its form (GmbH,
  GmbH & Co. KG, e.K., UG), the address, phone and e-mail, and names the Geschäftsführer (managing
  director). Take the company data from the Impressum; the Geschäftsführer may be used as
  `contactName`, no other private person.
- Keep umlauts and ß exactly as written (Aufzüge, Müller). Write `snippet` in English.
